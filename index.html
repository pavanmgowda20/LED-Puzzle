TRANSMISSION: INSTRUCTIONS

THE MISSION

Establish a closed electrical circuit by SLIDING wire tiles into empty sockets across the matrix. Tile wiring orientations are fixed and cannot be rotated. Power must flow continuously from (+), through the light bulb, and back to (-).

THE CONTROLS

  • Slide Tile: Click or tap any tile adjacent to an empty socket to slide it in.
  • Push Lines: Clicking a tile in the same row/column as an empty socket pushes all tiles in that line toward the socket!
  • Swipe & Drag: Drag/swipe tiles directly toward empty spaces on touch or mouse.
  • Keyboard Controls: Use Arrow Keys or WASD to slide tiles into the empty space.
  • Locked Obstacles: Identified by orange metal corner brackets. Locked tiles cannot be slid and must be routed around.
  • Instant Timer Save: The exact millisecond your circuit connects and lights the bulb, your completion time is automatically recorded and saved to the Relayathon Log!

TILE CLASSIFICATIONS

Straight: Direct path between opposite sides.
Corner: 90-degree redirection path.
Tee Junction: 3-way distribution junction.
Cross Junction: Independent 4-way crossing paths.
